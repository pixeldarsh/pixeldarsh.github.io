I'm a Class XI student at Amity International School, Pushp Vihar, New Delhi, with a deep interest in engineering, robotics, and autonomous systems. My academic focus is Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Computer Science (CBSE).

I scored **98.6%** in my CBSE Class X Board Examinations and continue to push well beyond the school syllabus — exploring embedded systems, control theory, and hardware projects independently.

**What drives me:** I believe that practical, dexterous robots can genuinely liberate people from repetitive and physically demanding work. Most current robotic systems lack the fine motor control needed for real-world tasks — that's the problem I want to spend my career solving.

My first real step in that direction was building a [Self-Stabilising Platform](/projects/self-stabilising-platform/) using a Raspberry Pi — integrating sensors, software, and physics into a working real-time control system. That project turned abstract ideas like feedback loops and control theory into something I could hold in my hands.

Outside engineering, I play **soccer and cricket** for my school team in inter-school competitions, and I've had the privilege of being well-travelled — giving me a broad, international perspective. I also provided editorial support for a published book on *Women CFO Stories*.
